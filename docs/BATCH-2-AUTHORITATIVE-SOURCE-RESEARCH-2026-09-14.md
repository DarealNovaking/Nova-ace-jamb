# Batch 2 — Authoritative Source Research — 2026-09-14

## Confirmed sources

- JAMB's official site exposes IBASS as its official Integrated Brochure and Syllabus System.
- The official IBASS Eligibility Checker models the required relationship as institution → programme → UTME subject combination, with Use of English selected by default.
- JAMB's official FAQ states that IBASS provides the available programmes in each tertiary institution and the UTME subject combination for each course, and directs candidates to verify requirements there.
- JAMB's Admissions Department states that it reviews/updates the UTME Brochure and liaises with NUC, NBTE and NCCE for quota-approved or delisted programmes.
- JAMB's Test Development Department states that it reviews and develops the UTME syllabus.
- JAMB's public registration-centre system currently exposes **2027 approved registration centres**, confirming that the 2027 cycle is already represented in official JAMB infrastructure.

## Product implication

The Batch 2 catalog pipeline is correctly designed around JAMB/IBASS as the authoritative source boundary. Institution/programme/UTME-rule data must not be invented or inferred from generic third-party lists.

The 2027 season remains a scaffold until authoritative 2027 exam composition/syllabus/programme data is actually published and verified. The presence of 2027 registration-centre infrastructure is not treated as evidence for 2027 UTME dates, question counts, subject rules or programme combinations.
