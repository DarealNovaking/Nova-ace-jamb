## Narrow proposed use basis

Primary authority checked: Nigerian Copyright Commission, Copyright Act2022, sections2(5) and3(a): https://www.copyright.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/CopyrightAct2023FinalPublication1.pdf . These provisions distinguish the underlying concepts/data from copyright in an eligible compilation and exclude ideas, concepts, principles and mere data. This supports the project's narrow assessment for factual concept names and ACE's own arrangement. It does not establish a JAMB licence, transfer rights in the official compilation or clear protected prose merely because the app is educational. Eligible government works can be protected; no blanket government-work exception is asserted. Fair dealing is not the chosen blanket justification.

The NCC's indexed registration FAQ corroborates the general distinction for titles/concepts, but refers to the older2004Act and direct retrieval encountered a redirect loop. The current2022 statute above is the authority used here. This is a documented internal scope assessment, not an external legal opinion or rights-holder permission.

**Included:** short factual study/concept labels, original grouping names and arrangement, links to official coverage references, internal source hashes/lineage for reconciliation.

**Excluded:** official PDF reproduction, source objective paragraphs, lessons, textbooks, literary texts, questions/answers, images, music/audio, and any claim that the index itself is an official JAMB publication. Annual reading lists and unresolved dates remain qualified. The user-selected2026 format does not establish that every annual reading list is unchanged.
